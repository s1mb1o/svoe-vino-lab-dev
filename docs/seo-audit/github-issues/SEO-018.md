SEO-018: Russian alcohol-advertising review of public copy before widening SEO content
<!-- labels: question -->

**Priority:** P2 · **Effort:** M · **Status:** REQUIRES VERIFICATION (legal/editorial)

## Problem

New SEO content (SEO-010 producer maps, SEO-011 explanatory text, SEO-030 titles) makes more alcohol-related information public. Federal Law 38-FZ art. 21 part 2 point 8 does not allow alcohol advertising on the Internet. The border between information and advertising depends on the actual copy and on the business relations of the service. No violation is asserted.

## Current state (verified on production, 2026-10-20)

- No numeric prices on checked public pages; producer maps show price tiers only («Доступный сегмент · ₽»).
- No «купить», «в корзину», «скидка», «акция» in page content; no `Product`/price `Offer` markup for wine.
- The footer warning «Чрезмерное употребление алкоголя вредит вашему здоровью» and «18+» are on every page; the age gate is on all non-legal pages.
- `webui/app/pages/terms.vue` (lines 39–51) describes informational use and no sales.

## Steps

- [ ] Before SEO-010 or SEO-011 ships: a Russian-law reviewer reads the actual copy and data of `/lineup/<producer>`, `ProductLineMap.vue`, the producer JSON, `WineExperiences.vue`, and the `/game`, `/crocodile`, `/ideas`, bot pages.
- [ ] Record page-specific decisions. Engineering checks them against SSR HTML, metadata, schema and outbound links.
- [ ] Rules until then: informational text only; no prices next to one wine, no calls to buy, no ranking of wines as «лучшее», no health-benefit claims, no paid search ads for alcohol terms.
