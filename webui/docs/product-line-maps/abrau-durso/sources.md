# Abrau-Durso catalog extension

Date: 2026-10-16.

## Scope

Keep the original six sheets, 52 product nodes and 14 groups.
Add 21 exact snapshot cards in five still-wine groups.
The extended map has 73 product nodes in 19 groups.
It represents 56 of the 57 producer snapshot cards through 53 canonical slugs and three verified aliases.
The 20 original gray products remain unchanged.
This is snapshot coverage. It is not complete current manufacturer assortment coverage.
The current official catalog includes later additions that are outside this task.

## Sources

- [Premium still wines](https://abrau.ru/collection/tihie-vina/premialnye-vina) identifies the Abrau-Durso collection and its premium positioning.
- [Abrau Estates](https://abrau.ru/collection/tihie-vina/abrau-estates/) identifies the contemporary collection.
- [The Estates introduction](https://vn.abrau.ru/news/abrau-durso-presents-abrau-estates-a-new-collection-of-still-wines) explicitly places the original collection in the medium-price bracket.
- [The local-grape release](https://abrau.ru/news/abrau-dyurso-predstavlyaet-abrau-estates-vinodelnya-yubileynaya) confirms Amurskiy Potapenko and Dostoynyy.
- [Abrau blends / Abrau Collection](https://abrau.ru/collection/tihie-vina/kupazhnye-vina) confirms the retro-car still-wine packages. Do not merge this group with sparkling Abrau Collection cans.
- [Az Abrau](https://abrau.ru/collection/tihie-vina/az-abrau) confirms the Azerbaijan origin, Bayanshira and Madrasa.
- [Femme d’Abrau](https://abrau.ru/collection/tihie-vina/abrau-femme) and [its release](https://abrau.ru/news/femme-d-abrau-kompliment-dlya-luchshey) confirm the collection and limited positioning.

Product names, descriptions and grape lists retain the saved snapshot values.
Current manufacturer blends can differ. Do not overwrite the historical card with current blend values.
The official manufacturer also warns that package appearance and blend composition can change.
Do not infer numeric price, year or alcohol from a slug suffix.
Keep similar names as separate cards unless exact equivalence is verified.
Az Abrau and Femme d’Abrau have a separate neutral tier. No common price rank is established.
The Abrau blend tier is editorial. Premium Abrau-Durso and medium Estates positioning have official collection evidence.

## One explicit exclusion

The card `rozovoe-polusladkoe-2` has no identified collection or bottle photograph.
Its source image is a generated wine-glass scene.
The owner requested that this card remain outside the map until exact identification.
The recognized-result fallback still shows this catalog result. It does not invent a product node, collection or price tier.
