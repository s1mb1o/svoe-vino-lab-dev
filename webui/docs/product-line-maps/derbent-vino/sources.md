# Derbent Vino product line sources

Checked on 2026-10-16.

The map contains every one of the 63 snapshot cards whose producer equals `Дербент Вино`. Each card has one node. All aliases are empty. Descriptions and grapes come from the saved snapshot. The map does not infer alcohol, sweetness, price, year, or aging from a slug. The snapshot color remains the style when no stronger card field exists.

## Collection evidence

- [Official catalog](https://derbentwine.ru/catalog/) confirms the producer brands.
- [Di Caspico](https://derbentwine.ru/catalog/dicaspico/) and [Fiori di Mare](https://derbentwine.ru/catalog/dicaspico/fiori-di-mare/) confirm separate collections.
- [Endemy](https://derbentwine.ru/catalog/endemy/) lists sparkling, still, and aged branches. [Sparkling Endemy](https://derbentwine.ru/catalog/endemy/igristye/) explicitly has premium positioning. A single Endemy group preserves uncertain snapshot package distinctions.
- [Desono](https://derbentwine.ru/catalog/desono/) confirms manual grape selection. Its relative tier is editorial.
- [Caucasian](https://derbentwine.ru/catalog/Caucasian/) confirms the collection. Its base tier is editorial.
- [Special projects](https://derbentwine.ru/catalog/spetsproekt/) confirms Monte Garu. [Graf Vorontsov](https://grafvorontsov.ru/) confirms its three wines and Charmat method.
- [5000](https://derbentwine.ru/catalog/spetsproekt/5000/) confirms three limited art packages. These packages are distinct from regular Desono cards.

## Additions and limits

The map adds three 5000 packages and Monte Garu rose semi-sweet. Their canonical wine slugs are null. All 63 snapshot names and slugs were checked for these products. `crosswalk.json` records each exact page URL and image URL.

The Monte Garu paragraph states three wines. The same page has four individually captioned images. The added rose semi-sweet product follows its explicit caption and distinct bottle image.

This is complete snapshot coverage with four verified additions. It is not a claim of complete coverage of the current official catalog. Other special-project brands are outside this verified subset.

Special releases and formats use a neutral tier. No numeric price is asserted. `catalog` records source evidence, not retail availability. The snapshot Bianca color conflicts with its name. The official [Bianca card](https://derbentwine.ru/catalog/endemy/igristye/bianka/) confirms white brut sparkling wine. The map corrects this field and shows a source correction badge.
